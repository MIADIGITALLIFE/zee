# ZEE Science Curriculum Governance

## Verified official source baseline

The current MOE source baseline identified for the P3–P6 Primary Science product is:

- **Title:** Science Teaching & Learning Syllabus — Primary Three to Six, Standard / Foundation.
- **Implementation:** Starting with the 2023 Primary Three cohort.
- **Document update:** May 2024.
- **Official PDF:** https://www.moe.gov.sg/-/media/files/primary/syllabus/primary-science-syllabus-2023_may24.pdf
- **Source pages:** The syllabus overview of level themes and topics is on printed page 21; the broader framework and Practices of Science appear earlier in the document.

The syllabus defines five Core Ideas themes — Diversity, Cycles, Systems, Energy and Interactions — alongside Practices of Science and Values, Ethics and Attitudes. ZEE must teach scientific thinking and inquiry, not just topic facts.

The official overview places P5 under **Systems / Interactions** and lists:
- Cycles in plants and animals (Reproduction)
- Cycles in matter and water (Water)
- Plant system (Respiratory and circulatory systems)
- Human system (Respiratory and circulatory systems)
- Electrical system

This is a syllabus-level topic overview, not yet a complete lesson/outcome import. The precise learning outcomes, track exclusions, and page-level mappings must be transcribed and reviewed before curriculum publication.

## Authority and scope

MOE syllabus documents define curriculum learning outcomes. SEAB publications define examination assessment arrangements where applicable. These sources must be stored separately and versioned separately.

P1–P2 content may be offered as a ZEE-designed science exploration pathway, but must not be labelled as official MOE Primary Science syllabus content unless an applicable official source explicitly supports that claim.

## Required provenance for every outcome

Each imported outcome must include:
- Official source title and URL.
- Syllabus/version identifier and effective period.
- Relevant cohort rule.
- Exact page or section reference.
- Level and Standard/Foundation applicability.
- Verification status and reviewer/date.

No generated lesson, question, rubric, or summary may be represented as an official MOE statement. Generated material must be labelled as ZEE teaching material mapped to a verified outcome.

## Cohort and track resolution

Do not select a syllabus solely from the learner's current primary level. Resolve the applicable syllabus using the learner's cohort year and the official transition rules recorded in `science_syllabus_versions.cohort_rule`. The 2023 syllabus document states that implementation starts with the 2023 Primary Three cohort. If a learner's applicable syllabus cannot be resolved confidently, set the syllabus as unconfirmed and request guardian confirmation.

The syllabus explicitly notes that topics underlined in its topic overview are not required for Foundation Science. The PDF's original formatting must be preserved when importing that distinction; do not infer Foundation scope from extracted plain text alone.

## Versioning

Published syllabus versions and lesson versions are immutable. Corrections create a new version; historical assessment results retain the syllabus, lesson, question, and rubric versions used at the time.

## Assessment governance

- Objective items may be marked deterministically.
- Structured answers must use an explicit mark-point rubric.
- Open responses use AI-assisted marking with confidence and rationale; low-confidence cases require human review.
- SEAB exam format metadata is versioned by year and must not be hard-coded as timeless curriculum truth.

For PSLE preparation, consult the current SEAB PSLE Formats Examined page separately from the MOE syllabus: https://www.seab.gov.sg/psle/psle-formats-examined-in-2026/
