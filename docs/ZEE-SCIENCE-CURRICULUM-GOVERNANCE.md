# ZEE Science Curriculum Governance

## Authority and scope

MOE syllabus documents define curriculum learning outcomes. SEAB publications define examination assessment arrangements where applicable. These sources must be stored separately and versioned separately.

P1–P2 content may be offered as a ZEE-designed science exploration pathway, but must not be labelled as official MOE Primary Science syllabus content unless an applicable official source explicitly supports that claim.

## Required provenance for every outcome

Each imported outcome must include:
- Official source title and URL.
- Syllabus/version identifier and effective period.
- Relevant cohort rule.
- Exact page or section reference.
- Level and Standard/Foundation applicability.
- Verification status and reviewer/date.

No generated lesson, question, rubric, or summary may be represented as an official MOE statement. Generated material must be labelled as ZEE teaching material mapped to a verified outcome.

## Cohort and track resolution

Do not select a syllabus solely from the learner's current primary level. Resolve the applicable syllabus using the learner's cohort year and the official transition rules recorded in `science_syllabus_versions.cohort_rule`. If the cohort cannot be resolved confidently, set the track/syllabus as unconfirmed and request guardian confirmation.

## Versioning

Published syllabus versions and lesson versions are immutable. Corrections create a new version; historical assessment results retain the syllabus, lesson, question, and rubric versions used at the time.

## Assessment governance

- Objective items may be marked deterministically.
- Structured answers must use an explicit mark-point rubric.
- Open responses use AI-assisted marking with confidence and rationale; low-confidence cases require human review.
- SEAB exam format metadata is versioned by year and must not be hard-coded as timeless curriculum truth.
