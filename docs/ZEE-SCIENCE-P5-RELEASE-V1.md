# ZEE Science — P5 Curriculum Release V1

## Release scope

This release adds the first publishable P5 curriculum slice to the ZEE Science platform. It is mapped to the Singapore MOE **Science Teaching & Learning Syllabus — Primary Three to Six, Standard / Foundation**, implementation starting with the 2023 Primary Three cohort, updated May 2024.

Official source:
https://www.moe.gov.sg/-/media/files/primary/syllabus/primary-science-syllabus-2023_may24.pdf

The MOE syllabus overview on printed page 21 identifies P5 under the Systems / Interactions themes and lists five topic areas. The detailed outcomes are mapped to these printed pages:

| P5 topic | Standard / Foundation outcome pages |
|---|---:|
| Cycles in Plants and Animals (Reproduction) | 43–47 |
| Cycles in Matter and Water (Water) | 49–51 |
| Human System (Respiratory and Circulatory Systems) | 54–55 |
| Plant System (Respiratory and Circulatory Systems) | 57–58 |
| Electrical System | 59–60 |

## What's seeded

- 10 lesson records: five topic areas × Standard and Foundation.
- 10 verified P5 outcome records, with the source pages and track-specific scope.
- 60 ZEE-authored multiple-choice assessment items: six per lesson.
- Each lesson includes English teaching sections, learning objectives, practice/test blueprints and a summary blueprint.
- Difficulty 1–3 items are used for guided practice; difficulty 4–5 items are reserved for the independent test.
- Answer keys remain server-side. The browser receives question prompts and options only.
- Assessment submissions must match the exact question IDs issued for that attempt.

## Important curriculum distinctions

- Reproduction Standard includes the cell as a basic unit of life, continuity of kind, inherited characteristics and the detailed plant/human fertilisation outcomes. Foundation follows its more limited stated outcomes.
- Water Standard includes investigation of factors affecting evaporation and water pollution impacts. Foundation uses the more limited recognition/state-change and conservation outcomes.
- Human System Standard compares plants, fish and humans and the transport of substances; Foundation uses the narrower plants/humans comparison.
- Plant System Standard requires identifying and describing transport-system parts; Foundation focuses on recognising the direction of water and food transport.
- Electrical System Standard includes bulbs in series and parallel; Foundation covers bulbs in series.

The syllabus notes that some content is not required for Foundation. ZEE lesson content and questions are constrained to the track-specific outcomes rather than simply reusing Standard questions.

## Teaching sequence

Every lesson follows the platform's five core learning stages, with an understanding check between teaching and practice:

1. Teaching — concept explanation and learning objectives.
2. Understanding check — learner self-check and teaching revisit if needed.
3. Practice — lower-difficulty questions.
4. Independent test — separate higher-difficulty question pool.
5. Summary — key ideas and recorded evidence.
6. Analysis — recorded strengths, learning gaps, confidence and next actions.

The user-facing product explicitly includes teaching, practice, testing, summary and analysis. The additional understanding check is a formative teaching checkpoint.

## Assessment boundaries

This initial P5 bank uses objectively marked MCQs so results can be deterministic. Written/structured-response questions, diagram labelling, data-response rubrics and experiment-design marking are not silently auto-scored; they will be added with explicit rubrics and human-review handling in a later release.

A single lesson score is not a mastery determination. Longitudinal mastery requires multiple evidence types and delayed recall / transfer checks.

## Publication boundary

Only P5 Standard and Foundation lessons are published by this migration. P1–P2 are ZEE-designed exploratory pathways, not official MOE syllabus levels. P3, P4 and P6 remain unavailable until their own outcome and content imports are reviewed.

All lesson explanations and assessment questions are ZEE-authored educational resources mapped to official MOE outcomes. They are not MOE publications or endorsed examination questions.
